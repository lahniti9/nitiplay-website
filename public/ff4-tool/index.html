What this app is (and is not)

  • FF4 Tool is a fan-made companion for Free Fire practice and setup planning (sensi, crosshair, HUD, aim training, optional AI Coach).
  • It is not affiliated with Garena or Free Fire.
  • It does not modify the Free Fire game client, provide aimbots, hacks, or unfair in-match advantages.
  • Settings you plan here must be entered manually inside Free Fire if you choose to use them.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login. Your setups and progress stay on your device.

Are there in-app purchases?

No. Version 1.0 is free, with no subscriptions.

Why is Coach unavailable?

FF4 Coach needs a working network connection. If Coach is temporarily unavailable, try again later. Never share passwords or personal contact details in chat.

How do I delete my data?

In the App go to Profile → Clear local data. You can also delete the App from your iPhone to remove local storage. To ask us to delete support emails you sent, email lahnitimoussa17@gmail.com.

How do I stop daily reminders?

Open Profile → Preferences and turn off daily reminders. You can also disable notifications for FF4 Tool in iOS Settings.

Photos permission

Photos access is only used when you choose to save a Practice Preview image. You can change permission anytime in iOS Settings → FF4 Tool.

Is this an official Garena app?

No. FF4 Tool is unofficial and fan-made. Free Fire trademarks belong to their owners.
