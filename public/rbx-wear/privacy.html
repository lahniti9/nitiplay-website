Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Style & Skins (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The App is published by Ismail Lahniti. Contact: lahnitimoussa17@gmail.com.

Style & Skins is a standalone avatar styling companion. We do not require an account and we do not ask you to log into a game through the App.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, email sign-up, or game login.
  • We do not collect your name, mailing address, or payment card details.
  • There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions in this version.
  • We do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores preferences locally on your device (for example using iOS storage APIs), which may include:

  • Onboarding answers you choose (style goals, preferences, display name you type)
  • Saved looks / favorites
  • Theme and language settings
  • Flags that you completed onboarding

This information stays on your device unless you back up the device with Apple. Deleting the App removes this local data.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads may include banners, interstitial ads on some taps, and optional rewarded ads (for example a quiz hint).

On iOS we may request App Tracking Transparency permission before personalized ads. You can decline. The App still works if you decline. Where required by law, we also show a consent form (UMP) before ads.

Google may process device identifiers, coarse location (if permitted), and ad interaction data according to Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob policies.

4. Catalog content from the internet

The App requests publicly available avatar catalog and thumbnail data over the internet so you can browse looks. Requests may include item identifiers needed to load images and list prices. We do not use this to log into a game account.

5. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App may schedule local reminders on your device (for example studio reminders you opted into). We do not send marketing push campaigns from our own servers.

6. Diagnostics

If you opt in to share diagnostics with Apple (through iOS Settings), Apple may provide crash or performance data according to your Apple privacy settings.

7. How we use information

  • Operate the App (load catalog looks, remember your settings, show ads)
  • Respond to support emails you send us
  • Comply with law and App Store rules

8. Sharing

We share information only with:

  • Apple — App Store services and, if you allow, diagnostics
  • Google AdMob — advertising as described above
  • Public catalog/thumbnail APIs — to load looks as described above
  • The host of this privacy page

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share data with data brokers except as needed for advertising partners described above if you allow tracking.

9. Retention

Local App data remains until you delete the App or clear it. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. Advertising partners retain data according to their policies.

10. Children

The App is intended for users 12 years and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal information to us, email lahnitimoussa17@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Your choices

  • Decline tracking when the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt appears
  • Use Privacy choices in the App (where shown) to manage ad consent
  • Decline optional notifications in iOS Settings
  • Delete the App to remove local data
  • Email us to request deletion of information you sent in support messages

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK, and certain US states), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you from support correspondence. Contact us to exercise those rights.

12. International processing

Apple, Google, and public catalog services may process data on servers outside your country. By using the App you understand this transfer is required to provide the service.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Effective date” at the top will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
