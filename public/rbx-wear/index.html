What this app is

  • Style & Skins is a standalone avatar styling companion: browse looks, build outfits in Look Studio, and save favorites.
  • It does not require an account or login.
  • It does not grant in-game items, real currency, or connect to a game account.
  • The in-app calculator is a virtual estimator for listed catalog prices. It does not process real money.
  • The app is free to use. Optional ads may appear.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions in this version. All features are included. You may see ads.

Common questions
